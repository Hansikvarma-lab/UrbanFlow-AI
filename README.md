
# 🚦 UrbanFlow-AI

## AI-Powered Smart Urban Mobility & Logistics System

UrbanFlow-AI is a smart urban mobility and logistics solution designed to
improve real-time monitoring, decision-making, and efficient management of
urban transportation and logistics using IoT, embedded systems, sensors,
and Artificial Intelligence.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Rapid urbanization leads to traffic congestion, inefficient mobility,
delays in transportation and logistics, and limited real-time monitoring.

Traditional systems often lack the ability to collect and process
real-time information and respond intelligently to changing urban
conditions.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

UrbanFlow-AI combines IoT-enabled sensors, ESP-based data acquisition,
Raspberry Pi 4 processing, and intelligent decision-making to create a
real-time smart urban mobility system.

The system collects data from connected sensors, transfers the required
information through the embedded communication system, processes the data,
and generates appropriate decisions or outputs.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Real-time monitoring of urban conditions
- Intelligent processing of sensor data
- Improve urban mobility and logistics efficiency
- Enable automated and intelligent decision-making
- Reduce delays and improve resource utilization
- Develop a scalable and cost-effective smart-city solution
